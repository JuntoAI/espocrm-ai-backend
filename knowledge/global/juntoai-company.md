# JuntoAI — Company Overview

## What We Do

JuntoAI builds AI-powered CRM intelligence for venture capital and startup ecosystems. We transform how investors manage deal flow, relationships, and portfolio companies through intelligent automation.

## Product

Our core product is an AI-native CRM layer that sits on top of EspoCRM, providing:
- **AI Assistant**: Natural language interface for CRM operations (search, create, update contacts/accounts/deals)
- **Proactive Intelligence**: Daily briefs, engagement decay alerts, relationship scoring
- **Deep Research**: Automated persona profiling of contacts via multi-pass web research
- **Smart Email Drafting**: Context-aware email composition using CRM data and personal communication style

## Differentiation

1. **CRM-native AI** — not a bolt-on chatbot, but deeply integrated into the CRM workflow
2. **Open-source foundation** — built on EspoCRM, no vendor lock-in
3. **European data sovereignty** — hosted on GCP Europe, GDPR-compliant by design
4. **Relationship intelligence** — goes beyond data entry to proactive relationship management

## Ideal Investor Profile

We're looking for investors who:
- Invest at Pre-Seed or Seed stage (€250K–€1.5M check sizes)
- Have experience in B2B SaaS, AI/ML, or CRM/Sales-tech
- Can provide strategic value: introductions to VC firms as customers, product feedback, go-to-market guidance
- Are based in DACH region or have strong European network (but open to global investors)

## Investment Criteria for Matching

When evaluating whether a VC is a good match for JuntoAI, consider:
- **Stage fit**: Do they invest at our stage (Pre-Seed/Seed)?
- **Sector fit**: Do they invest in B2B SaaS, AI, enterprise software, or fintech?
- **Check size**: Is their typical check size in our range?
- **Geographic fit**: Do they invest in European/DACH startups?
- **Value-add**: Can they open doors to VC firms as potential customers?
- **Portfolio conflicts**: Do they already have a competing CRM/AI investment?
