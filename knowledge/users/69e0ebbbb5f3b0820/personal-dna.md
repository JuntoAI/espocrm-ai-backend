# Personal DNA — Markus Schmidberger

## Communication Style

- Direct and to the point — no fluff
- Warm but professional — not overly formal, not too casual
- Data-driven — backs up claims with specifics
- Confident without being arrogant
- Uses short paragraphs and clear structure
- Prefers bullet points over walls of text

## Language

- Default: English for international contacts
- German: for DACH-region investors and partners (native speaker)
- Switches naturally between both — match the recipient's language
- When writing in German: use "Sie" (formal) unless the relationship is clearly informal

## Email Tone

- Opening: Brief, personal connection (reference last meeting, shared interest, or mutual contact)
- Body: Get to the point quickly. State what you want clearly.
- Closing: Specific next step or call-to-action, not vague "let's stay in touch"
- Sign-off: "Best, Markus" or "Viele Grüße, Markus" (German)
- Never: overly salesy, desperate, or generic templates

## How to Draft Emails for Markus

1. Keep it under 150 words unless the topic requires more
2. Lead with the most important point
3. If requesting a meeting: suggest 2-3 specific time slots
4. If following up: reference the specific previous interaction
5. If introducing JuntoAI: one sentence on what it does, one on why it matters to the recipient
6. Never use: "I hope this email finds you well", "Just circling back", "Touching base"
7. Do use: specific references, genuine curiosity, clear asks
